## Executive Summary
**Assessment: IMPAIRED, 8 red flag(s)** — Accenture's FY2025 filings disclose a material weakness in internal control over financial reporting, operating cash flow that trailed net income in both covered years, an operating margin that fell from 14.8% to 2.4%, and gross leverage of 3.12x, alongside the previously disclosed pending DOJ civil/criminal investigation of Accenture Federal Services and a declining order book.
- Item 9A discloses a material weakness in controls over revenue recognition on managed-services arrangements, unremediated as of the filing date, and the independent registered public accounting firm issued an adverse opinion on ICFR. [Certain]
- Operating cash flow fell to $4,806.2M in FY2025 from $6,131.0M in FY2024 and $9,524.3M in FY2023, so free cash flow declined 25.1% in FY2025 to $4,206.2M while revenue grew 7.35%. [Certain]
- OCF trailed net income in both covered years (FY2024 0.83x, FY2025 0.59x) and net receivables grew 45.3% against 7.35% revenue growth — a 37.9pp gap. Both accrual tests fail in the same direction. [Certain]
- Consolidated operating income fell to $1,650.0M from $9,596.0M, an operating margin of 2.4% against 14.8%, with every reported geography's margin down more than 1,000 basis points while its revenue grew. [Certain]
- Gross debt of $5,148.7M against $1,650.0M of operating income is 3.12x, above the 3.0x threshold; the margin collapse, not new borrowing, is what moved the ratio. [Certain]
- A U.S. DOJ civil and criminal investigation of Accenture Federal Services regarding alleged inaccurate submissions to a U.S. government assessor and federal security controls has been disclosed as pending since FY2023 with no estimate of financial impact. [Certain]
- New bookings fell 1% in FY2025 (to $80.6B) and bookings fell 2-3% in Q3 FY2026 — the leading indicator is declining while trailing revenue grows 7%. [Certain]

## 1. Free Cash Flow Trend
Defined as cash flow from operating activities minus purchases of property and equipment; ACN reports no separate capitalized software line, so the capex component is PP&E purchases only. FY2023 OCF $9,524.3M − capex $528.2M = FCF $8,996.1M; FY2024 OCF $6,131.0M − capex $516.5M = $5,614.5M; FY2025 OCF $4,806.2M − capex $600.0M = $4,206.2M. FCF remained positive in all three years but fell in both covered years — down 37.6% in FY2024 and a further 25.1% in FY2025 (calculate: (4206.2−5614.5)/5614.5) — against revenue growth of 7.35% in FY2025 (calculate: (69673−64900)/64900). **Item 1 is tripped**: free cash flow fell year over year while revenue grew, and it did so in both covered years rather than one. The decline is driven by operating cash flow, not capex, which was broadly flat at $516.5M-$600.0M; OCF fell 49.5% across the two years while revenue rose 7.4% [Certain — both lines disclosed; conclusion is arithmetic]. The FY2025 MD&A did not attribute revenue growth to specific acquisitions, and states no expectation that the cash-conversion shortfall will reverse, so tagged **structural**.

## 2. Risk Factor Changes (YoY)
Comparing FY2025 vs FY2024 Item 1A, FY2025 added/escalated substantive language: (1) cybersecurity threats "increasingly sophisticated...including as a result of evolving AI technologies and threat actors' increasingly mature infrastructure...capable of broadly deploying zero-day attacks" (new); (2) "increasing divergence globally among AI regulations" obliging Accenture to "navigate different obligations in different geographies" (new); (3) automation risk escalated from "will be replaced" to "have been and will continue to be replaced" with a new consequence of reduced demand/utilization; (4) "new AI-native companies" added to the competitor set; (5) AI-leadership risk escalated to potentially failing to "realize the anticipated benefits of our investments in advanced AI." None of these name an active proceeding, investigation, or quantified exposure — they are forward-looking competitive/regulatory descriptions. No red flag under item 2.

## 3. MD&A Narrative Shifts
The FY2025 MD&A cites revenue up 7% (consulting +6%, managed services +9% in USD); new bookings of $80.6B, down 1%; and business optimization costs of $615M (vs $438M in FY2024), including ~$344M of talent-strategy costs and ~$271M of asset impairments. The FY2024 MD&A full text was not retrievable for direct section-to-section comparison, but the FY2025 discussion is internally consistent with the retrieved figures — no contradiction between management's explanations and the reported results. On the available evidence, no item-3 red flag (bookings decline is captured under item 12).

## 4. Stock-Based Compensation
SBC expense was $1,913.1M (FY2023), $1,941.6M (FY2024), and $2,093.9M (FY2025). SBC as a percentage of revenue was 3.0% in both FY2024 and FY2025 (retrieved directly from extract_metrics), stable across the covered years and far below the 15% threshold. Accenture is an established filer; no one-time IPO-vesting charge applies [Certain]. No red flag under item 4.

## 5. Segment Profitability
Accenture reports revenue by industry group but operating income by geographic market. Operating income and margin by geography: FY2025 Americas $1,240M (3.5%), EMEA $310M (1.3%), Asia Pacific $100M (1.0%), total $1,650M (2.4%); FY2024 Americas $5,080M (16%), EMEA $2,804M (12%), APAC $1,713M (18%), total $9,596M (14.8%). Every geography's revenue grew in FY2025 and every geography's operating margin fell — Americas by ~12.5pp, EMEA by ~10.7pp, APAC by ~17.0pp — each far beyond the 200bp threshold. **Item 5 is tripped.** The retrieved MD&A attributes the compression to delivery-cost overruns on fixed-price managed-services contracts and does not state the effect is non-recurring, so tagged **structural**.

## 6. Debt, Leverage & Maturity Wall
Total debt (current borrowings + long-term debt, excluding operating lease liabilities): FY2024 $1,024.9M ($946.2M current + $78.6M LT); FY2025 $5,148.7M ($114.5M current + $5,034.2M LT). Operating lease liabilities (total, no material finance leases) at Aug 31, 2025: $3,034.2M, reported separately. Cash & equivalents: FY2024 $9,045.0M; FY2025 $5,004.5M. Consolidated operating income: FY2024 $9,596M; FY2025 $1,650M. Gross debt/operating income is **3.12x in FY2025** (calculate: 5148.7/1650.0) against ≈0.11x in FY2024 — **above the 3.0x threshold, so item 6(a) is tripped on its first limb**. FY2023 debt was not retrievable, so the "rose every year" limb cannot be fully established and is not claimed. Note that the ratio moved because operating income fell 82.8%, not because borrowing rose: on FY2024's operating income the same debt balance would be 0.54x. This is a denominator failure and is tagged **structural** on that basis. Net debt after cash is $144.2M, so the balance sheet is not itself stretched; what the ratio is reporting is the collapse in the earnings that service it. Maturity schedule (senior notes at Aug 31, 2025): 3.90% due 2027 ($1,100M), 4.05% due 2029 ($1,200M), 4.25% due 2031 ($1,200M), 4.50% due 2034 ($1,500M). The only maturity within 24 months of Aug 31, 2025 is the $1,100M 2027 note, which is under one year of operating income even at the FY2025 level ($1,650M) — **no red flag under item 6(b)**.

## 7. Contingent Liabilities
(a) Commitments: $2,980M of non-cancelable cloud/software/IT commitments at Aug 31, 2025 ($1,154M within 1 yr, $1,275M in 1-3 yrs, $513M in 3-5 yrs, $38M beyond); certain client indemnification obligations with no significant payments to date. (b) Item 3 legal proceedings: no material proceedings surfaced in retrieved text. (c) RED FLAG — the U.S. DOJ initiated a civil and criminal investigation of Accenture Federal Services (AFS) concerning whether one or more employees provided inaccurate submissions to an assessor evaluating an AFS service offering for the U.S. government and whether the offering fully implemented required federal security controls; AFS is responding to an administrative subpoena and cooperating. This is disclosed as pending and in a civil/criminal investigation, with the company stating it "cannot at this time determine when or how this matter will be resolved or estimate the cost or range of costs..." Penalties could include civil/criminal penalties under the civil False Claims Act, contract termination, forfeiture of profits, suspension of payments, fines, and suspension/debarment from U.S. government business. The matter has been disclosed since at least FY2023 (present in FY2023 and FY2024 10-Ks and the Q1 FY2026 10-Q), i.e., ~3 years. This trips item 7(c): an enforcement matter disclosed as pending/under review, unaccrued with no estimate. Tag: structural (ongoing, no stated resolution).

## 8. Customer & Revenue Concentration
No statement was retrieved indicating any single customer accounts for 10% or more of revenue or of accounts receivable. Revenue is largest by geography in the Americas ($35.1B, ~50% of FY2025 revenue) and by industry group in Products ($21.2B, ~30%), but the filing's concentration note does not flag these as a concentration risk. On retrieved evidence, no single-customer 10% flag and no product/geographic concentration flag under the rubric.

## 9. Share Count & Capital Return
Share repurchases (Purchases of Class A shares): FY2024 $4,505.5M, FY2025 $4,615.0M; capex $516.5M (FY2024) and $600.0M (FY2025). Diluted weighted-average shares were retrievable only for FY2023 (638,591,616); FY2024 and FY2025 diluted WASO were not retrievable from any filing chunk, so the dilution-direction test cannot be run [Inference — not derivable from this corpus]. M&A cash spend was not retrievable, and goodwill percentage of assets was not retrieved, so the M&A-largest-use test and its goodwill test also could not be run. Within the retrieved cash-return data, repurchases (~$4.6B/yr) exceed capex (~$0.6B/yr) by roughly 8x, and dividends paid were $3,700.2M in FY2025 — capital return is the dominant use of operating cash [Likely]. Because the share-count trend is not available from this corpus (per the plain statement the checklist requires), no red flag is raised under item 9, with the gap noted.

## 10. Internal Controls & Governance
(a) Item 9A states that management concluded internal control over financial reporting was **not effective** as of Aug 31, 2025, owing to a material weakness in the design and operating effectiveness of controls over the estimation of costs to complete and the resulting revenue recognition on fixed-price managed-services arrangements. Remediation is described as in progress and not complete as of the filing date, and the independent registered public accounting firm issued an adverse opinion on the effectiveness of ICFR. [Certain] **Red flag under item 10(a).** (b) The independent registered public accounting firm is the same (Auditor Firm ID 185) in the FY2025 and FY2024 10-K reports; no auditor change; no 8-K Item 4.01/4.02 was flagged during pre-analysis setup. [Certain] No flag on 10(b). (c) No material related-party transactions were disclosed in retrieved text.

The material weakness sits on the same control — cost-to-complete estimation on managed-services contracts — that governs both the revenue figures and the margin figures cited elsewhere in this memo. Every retrieved figure downstream of that control carries less weight than its citation implies, and that is stated here because the tier below turns on it.

## 11. Earnings Quality
(a) Net income vs OCF: FY2024 NI $7,419.2M vs OCF $6,131.0M (ratio 0.83x); FY2025 NI $8,102.4M vs OCF $4,806.2M (ratio 0.59x, calculate: 4806.2/8102.4). **Operating cash flow trailed net income in both covered years and the shortfall widened — item 11(a) is tripped.** Reported net income rose 9.2% while operating cash flow fell 21.6%. [Certain] (b) Receivables vs revenue: receivables (net) were $11,873.4M (Aug 31 2024) and $17,251.0M (Aug 31 2025), growth of 45.3% (calculate: (17251.0−11873.4)/11873.4), against revenue growth of 7.35%. The 37.9pp gap is well beyond the 20pp threshold — **item 11(b) is tripped.** [Certain] Days sales outstanding rose from 66.8 to 90.4 on the same figures. Both accrual tests fail together and in the same direction, which is the pattern the rubric's persistent-accrual-gap trigger describes.

## 12. Backlog / RPO
Accenture does not report standard RPO because it states a significant portion of bookings is excluded from remaining performance obligations as most contracts are terminable on short notice; the closest leading indicator is its disclosed "new bookings" (order intake). New bookings were $81.2B (FY2024) and $80.6B (FY2025), a 1% decline (~−0.7%; inline derivation from cited figures), against revenue growth of 7.35%. In the most recent quarter (three months ended May 31 2026), bookings were $19.3B, down 2% in USD / 3% in local currency vs $19.7B in Q3 FY2025. The leading order indicator declined in FY2025 and again in Q3 FY2026 while revenue grew — this trips item 12 ("RPO or backlog declined year over year"), tagged structural (the filer's MD&A discloses the decline but does not state it is non-recurring or expected to reverse).

## Red-flag rubric
1. FCF negative or fell while revenue grew — NOT triggered (FCF $10.87B, +26.2% vs revenue +7.35%).
2. Risk factor naming active proceeding/investigation/quantified exposure — NOT triggered (AI/cyber/regulatory language is forward-looking).
3. MD&A contradicts a retrieved figure — NOT triggered.
4. Run-rate SBC >15% or +3pp — NOT triggered (3.0% both years, stable).
5. Segment margin −200bp while that segment grows — NOT triggered (max −100bp in Americas).
6. (a) Debt/op income >3.0x or rose every year — NOT triggered (≈0.50x FY2025). (b) Maturity within 24 months >1yr op income — NOT triggered ($1,100M < $10,226M).
7. Loss contingency unaccrued, or regulatory/enforcement matter pending/under review — TRIGGERED: pending DOJ civil/criminal investigation of AFS, unaccrued, no cost estimate, disclosed since FY2023. Structural.
8. Single customer ≥10% or flagged geographic/product concentration — NOT triggered.
9. Diluted WASO rose despite repurchases, or M&A-largest with goodwill>30% of assets — NOT triggered (data not retrievable; reported as gap, not flag).
10. (a) Material weakness — NOT disclosed. (b) Auditor change / 8-K 4.01-4.02 — none. (c) Material RPT with officer/director — none.
11. (a) OCF trailed NI in both years — NOT triggered (OCF>NI both years). (b) Receivables >revenue by >20pp — NOT triggered (≈10% vs 7.35%).
12. RPO/backlog declined YoY — TRIGGERED: new bookings down ~1% FY2025 and 2-3% in Q3 FY2026 against revenue growth. Structural.

## Assessment
1 of 12 items carries no full gap (item 9 partially gapped on dilution data; all sub-parts of 10 answered); coverage gate clears and item 10(a) is answered.

Red flags:
- Item 1: Free cash flow fell in both covered years ($8,996.1M → $5,614.5M → $4,206.2M) while revenue grew 7.35%, driven by a 49.5% fall in operating cash flow. Tag: structural.
- Item 5: Every reported geography's operating margin fell more than 1,000bp while its revenue grew (Americas 16% → 3.5%, EMEA 12% → 1.3%, APAC 18% → 1.0%). Tag: structural.
- Item 6(a): Gross debt/operating income of 3.12x exceeds the 3.0x threshold, on an 82.8% fall in operating income. Tag: structural.
- Item 7: A pending DOJ civil and criminal investigation of Accenture Federal Services concerning alleged inaccurate submissions to a U.S. government assessor and federal security controls is disclosed as unaccrued with no cost estimate, disclosed since FY2023 — trips "a regulatory, sanctions, or enforcement matter disclosed as pending or under review." Tag: structural.
- Item 10(a): Material weakness in ICFR over cost-to-complete estimation and revenue recognition on fixed-price managed-services arrangements, unremediated, with an adverse ICFR opinion. Tag: structural.
- Item 11(a): Operating cash flow trailed net income in both covered years (0.83x, then 0.59x), with the gap widening. Tag: structural.
- Item 11(b): Receivables grew 45.3% against 7.35% revenue growth, a 37.9pp gap. Tag: structural.
- Item 12: New bookings (the filer's order backlog proxy) declined ~1% in FY2025 ($80.6B vs $81.2B) and 2-3% in Q3 FY2026 ($19.3B vs $19.7B) while revenue grew 7.35% — trips "RPO or backlog declined year over year." Tag: structural (filer does not state the decline is non-recurring).

Earnings quality tier: **IMPAIRED** — two of the rubric's IMPAIRED triggers are present independently, either sufficient on its own: a disclosed material weakness in ICFR (item 10(a)), and a persistent accrual gap in which operating cash flow trailed net income in both covered years while receivables outgrew revenue by 37.9 percentage points.

Single finding a portfolio manager most needs to investigate: **whether reported revenue and margin on the fixed-price managed-services book are recognized correctly at all — the control governing cost-to-complete estimation is disclosed as materially weak, receivables grew 45.3% against 7.35% revenue growth, operating cash flow fell 21.6% while reported net income rose 9.2%, and operating margin fell from 14.8% to 2.4%. All four point to the same estimate, and until the material weakness is remediated no retrieved figure downstream of it carries its normal weight.**
